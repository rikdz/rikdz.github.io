My research focuses on new methods for reasoning from data, human-computer
collaboration, and improving evaluation of AI systems. Previously, I worked on
those same things plus question answering, language grounding, and pragmatic
meaning representation.

I completed my PhD in Computational Linguistics at the University of Washington
in 2019, advised by [Hanna Hajishirzi](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~hannaneh/)
and [Gina-Anne Levow](https://faculty.washington.edu/levow/). Before Apple I
was a postdoctoral researcher with [Noah Smith](https://nasmith.github.io/) at
UW CSE, an Applied Scientist at Amazon, and a Research Scientist at
[S&P Global Kensho](https://www.kensho.com/).
